One workspace

From recording to a transcript you can use.

Transcribe files, keep track of meetings, and shape the result without leaving your Mac.

Transcribe audio and video

Choose a Whisper model and language, follow the transcript as it appears, then export TXT, SRT, or WebVTT.

Process batches

Queue multiple files, track progress, retry failures, and open the exported transcripts beside their source media.

Edit speakers and segments

Rename speakers, correct transcript segments, search and replace text, and keep playback synced with the words.

Capture meetings and make notes

Record microphone and system audio, then generate a local overview, decisions, key points, and action items.

Private by design

Your recordings stay yours.

Transcription, transcript chat, and meeting summaries run locally. Model files download separately when first needed; your audio and transcripts stay on your Mac.
